Nbody2D.PS_DTFE — Type
PS_DTFE(state_initial, state_final, box; G = n)
PS_DTFE(state_initial, state_final, depth, box)

Phase-space Delaunay tessellation field estimator. The particles are connected by the regular triangulation of their Lagrangian lattice (lattice_triangles), every triangle is unwrapped across the periodic boundaries on its own (unwrap_triangle), and triangles crossing the edge of the box are stored together with their periodic copies.

Triangles are located with a uniform G×G cell index (GridIndex); by default G is the size n of the particle lattice. The second form, with a depth, is kept for compatibility: the depth is not used for the queries, and a bounding volume hierarchy for debugging can be built with BVH(estimator, depth).

Query points are taken modulo the box. A point lying exactly on an edge or vertex of the tessellation is counted in every triangle that contains it. Fields can be evaluated at arbitrary points (ps_density, numberOfStreams, ps_velocity, velocitySum) or, much faster, on regular grids (ps_density_grid, numberOfStreams_grid).

Nbody2D.lattice_triangles — Method
lattice_triangles(N)

Triangulation of the periodic N×N Lagrangian lattice. Every cell is split along the same diagonal, into (i, j)–(i+1, j)–(i+1, j+1) and (i, j)–(i+1, j+1)–(i, j+1), so that every particle is a vertex of exactly six triangles. Particle (i, j) has index i + (j - 1) N, matching reshape(state.position, :, 2).

Nbody2D.rasterise — Method
rasterise(estimator, xs, ys)

Density and number of streams on the regular grid xs × ys (rows y, columns x), by looping once over the triangles and over the grid points inside each of them. The grid may extend beyond the box: every triangle is placed at all its periodic images that overlap the grid, so the result equals the point queries at mod.(p, L).

Nbody2D.unwrap_s — Method
unwrap_s(s, L)

Cosmological N-body simulations normally work with periodic boundary conditions. This function undoes the wrapping of particles around the box in the displacement field, assuming the particles did not travel more than half the size of the box.

source
Nbody2D.unwrap_triangle — Method
unwrap_triangle(x, sim, L)

Eulerian corners of the triangle with particle indices sim (rows of x). The second and third corners are placed at the periodic image nearest to the first corner, and the first corner is shifted into [0, L)². This only assumes that the edges of the triangle are shorter than L/2, so particles may have wrapped around the box any number of times.
